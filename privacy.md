# EasyPark Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: August 13, 2026**

EasyPark respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you provide while using the EasyPark mobile application.

## Information We Collect

EasyPark may collect the email address and account information you provide when creating or using an EasyPark account.

With your permission, EasyPark may access your approximate or precise device location. Location information is used to provide map and parking-related functionality, such as displaying your position and helping you locate nearby parking locations.

EasyPark may also store information related to parking activity, such as parking selections, bookings, or other information necessary to provide the application's features.

## How We Use Your Information

The information collected by EasyPark is used to authenticate users, provide parking and map functionality, manage parking-related activity and bookings, maintain application functionality, and improve the user experience.

EasyPark does not sell users' personal information.

## Location Information

EasyPark requests location permission only when location-based functionality is required. Users may control location permission through their Android device settings.

Disabling location permission may prevent some map or nearby-parking features from working correctly.

## Third-Party Services

EasyPark uses Google Maps Platform services to provide map and location-related functionality.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and security practices.

## Data Security

EasyPark takes reasonable measures to protect user information and uses secure network connections when transmitting user data to supported online services.

## Data Retention

Account information and associated EasyPark data may be retained while the user's account remains active.

When a verified account deletion request is received, EasyPark will delete the user's account and associated personal data within a reasonable period, normally within 30 days.

If backup copies exist, they may remain temporarily until the normal backup cycle removes them. Information that must be retained for legitimate security or legal purposes may be retained only for as long as necessary.
