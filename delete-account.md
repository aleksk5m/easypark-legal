# EasyPark Account Deletion

**Last Updated: August 13, 2026**

EasyPark users may request deletion of their EasyPark account and the personal data associated with that account.

## Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your EasyPark account, send an email to:

**[Akmitrovski5@gmail.com]**

Use the subject:

**EasyPark Account Deletion Request**

Send the request from the email address associated with your EasyPark account whenever possible.

In your request, provide the email address associated with the EasyPark account you want deleted.

Do **not** send your password.

EasyPark may contact you to verify that you are the owner of the account before completing the deletion request.

## What Will Be Deleted

After the request has been verified, EasyPark will delete the user's EasyPark account information, email/account profile information controlled by EasyPark, saved parking or booking information associated with the account, and other personal application data associated with the account that EasyPark controls.

Location information stored by EasyPark, if any, and associated with the account will also be deleted where applicable.

## Data Retention

Verified account deletion requests will normally be completed within 30 days.

If temporary backup copies of account information exist, those copies may remain until the applicable backup cycle expires.

EasyPark may retain limited information when necessary for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, or legal requirements. Such information will only be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for that purpose.

## After Account Deletion

Account deletion is permanent. After the account has been deleted, the user may need to create a new EasyPark account to use features that require authentication.

Deleting an EasyPark account does not delete information controlled independently by third-party services or the user's Google account.
